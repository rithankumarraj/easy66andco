📌 How to get Meta WhatsApp Sandbox credentials:
  1. Go to developers.facebook.com and sign in.
  2. Create or select your App → Add WhatsApp product.
  3. Under WhatsApp → API Setup, you will see a temporary Access Token and Phone number ID.
  4. In the To field in Meta Console, add and verify your personal phone number using an OTP to whitelist it in Sandbox mode.
  5. Paste your Phone Number ID, Access Token, and verified recipient number below.
